Hand in the one plan you were asked to write, ask another author, or withdraw your plan because it is the same as another one. This tool is for plan authors during an exploration; the main agent opens decision points with Trajectory instead.

You were given one angle. Investigate what that angle needs within the step budget in your project context, then `submit` exactly one plan: its `title`, the `basis` from what you read, its main `risk`, `fit` — one line on the situation it suits — the `prompt` that starts it, and the full `detail` in Markdown. Add `caution` only when the plan has a concrete problem worth warning the user about. You do not have to read the other plans. Only when you are unsure about something another author may have settled, `ask` them (`to` is their key, or `all`) and carry on without waiting: a reply, if any, shows up among your messages in a later step. If you find your plan is the same as another one, `withdraw` it, name that plan in `sameAs` and say why in `reason`.

You never create or change project files, run shell commands, or ask the user; when a fact is missing, write the assumption into your plan. The submitted plan is written to a file in the project, and its path comes back in the result. Once your plan is submitted or withdrawn, end your turn with one short line.
